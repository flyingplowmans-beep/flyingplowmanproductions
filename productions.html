YOU

by Mark Wilson

In November 2025, Flying Plowman Productions presented YOU by Mark Wilson in association with Coracle Arts at The Seven Dials Playhouse in London’s Covent Garden as part of their ‘Origins’ season.

The play

YOU is a powerful play about separation, longing and the invisible ties that bind us.

Now in her mid 40s, Kathleen sits anxiously awaiting the arrival of the man she was forced to give up for adoption more than 30 years ago. As she finally allows herself to remember the events of that time, her story comes to life in a beautifully woven two-hander by BBC Radio writer Mark Wilson.

Touring Spring 2027

YOU by Mark Wilson will be touring on the rural touring network in Spring 2027. If your venue is interested in this production, please get in touch!
